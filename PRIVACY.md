# SetApart Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 30, 2026**

SetApart ("we," "us," "our") provides a mobile and web application for
watching and, for approved organizations, publishing Christian video
content. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use
it, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your
display name, email address, and password (handled securely by
Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password
ourselves).

**Usage data.** To provide features like favorites, watch history, and
following organizations, we store:
- Videos you've marked as favorites or saved to watch later
- Your watch history and playback progress
- Organizations you follow

**Organization application information.** If you apply to register an
organization, we collect the organization's name, description, contact
email, and optionally a location and website URL, in order to review
and verify the application.

**Uploaded content.** Approved organizations can upload video files and
associated metadata (title, description, category, tags, speaker name).
Video files themselves are hosted and streamed by Mux, our video
infrastructure provider — see "Third-Party Services" below.

**We do not currently collect:** precise location, contacts, payment
information, or any advertising/analytics identifiers. SetApart does
not display third-party ads.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:
- Operate and maintain your account and the app's core features
- Review and moderate organization applications and video submissions
- Communicate with you about your application, account, or submitted
  content
- Maintain the security and integrity of the service

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Third-Party Services

SetApart relies on the following service providers, each of which
processes data on our behalf under their own privacy and security
practices:

- **Firebase (Google)** — authentication, database storage, and backend
  functions. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Mux** — video hosting, transcoding, and streaming for uploaded
  content. See [Mux's Privacy Policy](https://mux.com/privacy).

## Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information and usage data for as long as your
account is active. You may request deletion of your account and
associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. We
will delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable time, except
where we are required to retain it by law.

## Children's Privacy

SetApart is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a
child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and
we will delete it.

## Your Choices

- You can review and update your account information from within the
  app's Profile screen.
- You can unfollow organizations, remove favorites, and clear watch
  history from within the app.
- You can request a copy of your data or request account deletion by
  contacting us (see below).

## Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information,
including Firebase Authentication for account security and Firestore
Security Rules to restrict who can read or write data. No method of
transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee
absolute security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last
updated" date above when we do. Continued use of SetApart after a
change means you accept the revised policy.
